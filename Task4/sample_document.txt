SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------------------

ACME Technologies is a leading software development company founded in 2015 in Bangalore, India. 
We specialize in cloud computing solutions, artificial intelligence, and mobile application development.
Our mission is to deliver innovative technology solutions that transform businesses globally.

Current Employee Strength: 2,500+ employees across 5 offices
Headquarters: Bangalore, Karnataka
Branch Offices: Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune

SECTION 2: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
---------------------------------------

Standard Working Hours:
- Regular employees: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Monday to Friday)
- Lunch Break: 1 hour (1:00 PM to 2:00 PM)
- Total working hours per week: 40 hours

Flexible Working:
- Employees can opt for flexible working hours between 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM arrival
- Work from home is allowed 2 days per week with manager approval
- Hybrid model: 3 days office, 2 days remote

Attendance Policy:
- Minimum 90% attendance required per month
- Late arrivals (after 10:00 AM) require prior approval
- More than 3 unauthorized absences may result in disciplinary action

---

SECTION 3: LEAVE POLICY
-----------------------

Annual Leave Entitlements:
1. Casual Leave: 12 days per year
2. Sick Leave: 12 days per year
3. Earned Leave: 18 days per year (can be carried forward)
4. Maternity Leave: 26 weeks (paid)
5. Paternity Leave: 2 weeks (paid)

Special Leave:
- Marriage Leave: 5 days
- Bereavement Leave: 3 days (immediate family)

Leave Application Process:
- Apply through HR portal at least 3 days in advance
- Emergency leave requires manager notification within 2 hours
- Leave approval is discretionary and based on project requirements

Unused Leave:
- Casual and sick leave cannot be carried forward
- Earned leave can be carried forward up to 30 days
- Encashment available for earned leave at end of financial year

SECTION 4: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
------------------------------------

Salary Structure:
- Basic Salary: 40% of CTC
- House Rent Allowance (HRA): 30% of CTC
- Special Allowance: 20% of CTC
- Performance Bonus: 10% of CTC (annual, performance-based)

Salary Payment:
- Monthly salary credited on last working day of the month
- Payslips available on employee portal
- Annual increment review in April each year

Benefits:
1. Health Insurance:
   - Employee coverage: ₹5 lakhs
   - Family coverage: ₹5 lakhs (spouse and 2 children)
   - Parents coverage: ₹3 lakhs (optional, at employee cost)

2. Accidental Insurance: ₹10 lakhs coverage

3. Provident Fund (PF):
   - Employee contribution: 12% of basic salary
   - Employer contribution: 12% of basic salary

4. Gratuity: Payable after 5 years of continuous service

5. Performance Bonus:
   - Quarterly performance bonus up to 20% of quarterly salary
   - Annual performance bonus based on company and individual performance

Other Perks:
- Laptop and mobile phone for business use
- Internet and mobile reimbursement: ₹2,000 per month
- Professional development budget: ₹50,000 per year
- Gym membership reimbursement: ₹5,000 per year

SECTION 5: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
-----------------------------------

Learning Opportunities:
- 40 hours of mandatory training per year
- Access to online learning platforms (Udemy, Coursera, Pluralsight)
- Technical certification reimbursement up to ₹1,00,000
- Conference attendance: 2 conferences per year (domestic)

Career Growth:
- Performance review every 6 months
- Promotion eligible after 18 months in current role
- Internal job postings available to all employees

Mentorship Program:
- New employees assigned a mentor for first 6 months
- Regular one-on-one sessions with managers
- Career counseling sessions quarterly

SECTION 6: CODE OF CONDUCT
--------------------------

Professional Behavior:
- Maintain professional decorum at all times
- Respect diversity and inclusion
- Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination
- Confidentiality of company and client information is mandatory

Dress Code:
- Business casual attire Monday to Thursday
- Casual attire on Fridays
- Formal attire for client meetings

Security and Compliance:
- All employees must wear ID cards in office premises
- Visitor access requires prior approval
- No unauthorized photography or recording in office
- Data security policies must be strictly followed

Conflict of Interest:
- Employees must disclose any external business interests
- No moonlighting or working for competitors
- Intellectual property created during employment belongs to company

---

SECTION 7: TERMINATION AND EXIT PROCESS
---------------------------------------

Notice Period:
- Employees: 2 months notice required
- Company: 2 months notice or pay in lieu
- Probation period: 1 month notice from either side

Exit Process:
- Submit resignation through HR portal
- Exit interview with HR mandatory
- Complete knowledge transfer to team
- Return all company assets (laptop, ID card, access cards)
- Full and final settlement within 45 days of last working day

Relieving Documents:
- Relieving letter issued after successful exit process
- Experience certificate provided upon request
- No objection certificate (NOC) for visa purposes

----------------------------------------

Employee Assistance Program:
